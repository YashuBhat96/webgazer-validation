Webcam eye tracking

Gaze Check

Welcome to the WebGazer eye tracking validation study. You will set up your webcam, complete a 12 point calibration, look at 25 accuracy locations twice (50 presentations), practice finding letters, and complete a separate 50 trial letter search task.

Estimated total time: about 15–25 minutesMost of the time is spent setting up the camera and finding letters. It may take longer if you need a retry or more time to find targets. We will show an estimate before each step.

Privacy notice

Webcam images are used temporarily in your browser to estimate where you are looking. We do not save or upload webcam photos, video, or face images. The study collects predicted gaze positions, timestamps, calibration and validation measurements, target positions, clicks and response times, browser and device information, and any questionnaire answers you provide.

Results are held only in this open browser tab during the task, not saved in persistent browser storage. They are sent over HTTPS to the study server only when you choose Submit Study Data. If you refresh or close the tab before submitting, those unsent results will be lost. The research team can view and export those records in a password protected dashboard. You may stop participating at any time. If you do not submit, do not press the final submission button.

Entering your assigned participant ID and checking the box below confirms that you have read this information and agree to participate and submit the study data. This short notice is intended for an IRB approved study; it does not replace any longer consent materials required by the study protocol.

You’ll need: a bank or ID card (to measure your screen) and a tape measure or ruler (to measure how far you sit from the camera). At the end, press the large Submit Study Data button to send your records to the research server. If this is a local pilot, download a backup instead.

Your participant ID and agreement acknowledgment are included in the study submission. Do not enter your name, email address, phone number or another personal identifier. A random technical session ID is also assigned automatically.

Set up · 5 of 5

Set yourself up well

Allow about 1–3 minutes for camera permission and positioning. Once ready, calibration takes about 30 seconds.

Webcam eye tracking is only as good as the setup. These five things make the biggest difference:

  1. Raise your webcam to eye level, pointing straight at your face. Prop your laptop on some books if you need to.
  2. Light on your face, not behind you. Face a window or lamp; avoid a bright window behind you.
  3. Glasses off if you can see the screen clearly without them; reflections on the lenses confuse the tracker. If you can’t, keep them on. Contact lenses are fine, so keep them in.
  4. Sit 50–70 cm from the screen, about an arm’s length, and stay in that position until everything is finished. Keep your head still.
  5. Close other heavy apps and tabs. The tracker needs the processing power.

    Your data

    WebGazer protocol adapted from Brand, Diamond, Thomas & Gilbert-Diamond (2021), Behavior Research Methods, 53(4), 1502–1514. The independent accuracy assessment uses 25 distinct screen positions (the original 12-point 4×3 Brand-style subset plus 13 additional positions), shown in two separately randomized passes (50 presentations total), with 2000 ms targets, 250 ms blank intervals, and exclusion of the first 100 ms. Calibration uses a separate 12-point WebGazer training grid. The visual search task has 10 practice trials and 50 study trials. The E and # display is proportionally reduced from Brand’s 165×245 CSS-pixel bordered reference stimulus when necessary so that all 18 bordered items remain separated; its scale and viewport are recorded per trial. The webcam implementation is not an exact replication of the GP3 hardware protocol. Primary accuracy is mean gaze offset and primary precision is RMS spatial dispersion; sample-level error and successive-point RMS are also exported. Viewing distance: the participant's tape measurement when given; otherwise estimated from the distance between the eyes in WebGazer's face mesh (average adult interpupillary distance, assumed camera field of view).

    Your data

    Visual-search task adapted from Brand et al. (2021): 10 practice and 50 experimental trials, each with 2 targets and 16 distractors. Correct target clicks provide the operational ground-truth proxy within 500 ms, not independent eye movement ground truth; fixations via an Engbert & Kliegl (2003) velocity detector (λ=6, median-based SD); TP/FP/FN/TN scored within a 500 ms click-linked events across eight AOI sizes and 100/150/275 ms minimum fixation durations. WebGazer stimulus dimensions differ from the GP3 study; comparisons remain methodological, not direct performance equivalence.
